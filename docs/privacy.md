# Firefly Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 17, 2026**

Firefly is a Philips Hue light controller for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Mac. It is designed so that your data never leaves your home.

## What we collect

Nothing. Firefly has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

## What stays on your device

Firefly stores the following locally on your device only:

- Your Hue Bridge address and the credentials the bridge issues when you pair
- Cached room, zone, and scene data from your bridge
- Your preferences (such as pinned scenes)

This data is never sent anywhere except directly to your own Hue Bridge, and you can erase it at any time by disconnecting the bridge in the app or deleting the app.

## Network connections

Firefly talks to exactly two things:

1. **Your Hue Bridge**, over your local home network, to discover and control your lights.
2. **Philips Hue's discovery service** (`discovery.meethue.com`), which helps find your bridge's address on your network. This request contains no personal information.

## Third parties

There are none. No third-party SDKs, no data sharing, no data sales.
